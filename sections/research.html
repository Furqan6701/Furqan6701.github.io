Research Interests

Areas of academic and professional focus in geospatial science.

I'm now moving toward the more automated, AI-driven side of geospatial work, particularly GeoAI, advanced computer vision, and Google Earth Engine. These are areas I'm actively drawn to but haven't worked on in a formal capacity yet. I'm also building toward interactive web-based mapping, extending the web development skills I already have into more spatial, map-driven interfaces.

My goal is to apply machine learning to feature extraction and change detection on satellite imagery, and to run earth observation analysis at scale in the cloud rather than in desktop GIS software.
