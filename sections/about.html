About Me

Academic background and professional interests in geospatial science.

I'm a Geoinformatics Engineering student at NUST in Islamabad, currently working as a GIS Developer at The Map Ventures. Most of my time goes into satellite imagery, spatial analysis, and building tools that turn raw geospatial data into something people can actually use.

On the technical side, I split my time between GIS software like ArcGIS and QGIS and writing Python to automate the parts that shouldn't be done by hand. I've built cadastral digitization pipelines, route-optimization prototypes, and image-processing tools that lean on OpenCV and Sentinel-2 data. A full breakdown of what I use day to day is in the Skills section below.

Outside of client work, I'm teaching myself GeoAI, computer vision, and Google Earth Engine. None of that is on my CV yet, but it's where I want to spend more of my time: automated feature extraction from satellite imagery, running large-scale earth observation analysis in the cloud, and connecting computer vision models directly to geospatial pipelines.
